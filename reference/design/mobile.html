1. Colours (20, And No Others)

2. Rules

  1. One meaning per colour: blue = start something, green = finish / money in, red = delete / money out, orange = credit, slate = draft / cancelled.
  2. Every amount also has an arrow or a word: ↓ to get, ↑ to give.
  3. Every button is at least 44 px tall, easy for a finger.
  4. Names (party, supplier, item) always stay on one line; what does not fit ends with "…". Amounts are never cut; they get a little smaller when space is short.
  5. Party rows have no letter circle before the name; the name starts the row, like a bill row.
  6. Dates look the same everywhere: 25 Sep 2026 (day, short month, year).
  7. A transaction row shows its name first (Sale Bill, Payment In), and under it the number | date: INV-0107 | 14 Nov 2026.
  8. Every word of a button, field, tab, badge or heading starts with a capital letter: Purchase Bill, Take Payment. Full sentences stay normal.
  9. Delete is always light red, never solid red, and always asks first.
  10. New look: buttons have a soft shine and shadow and press down when tapped; cards are slightly raised. Shadows and shine are made from the same 20 colours, never a new colour.
  11. Portrait only. Light look only.

3. Lettering

Plus Jakarta Sans for English (the same as the computer app), Noto Sans Devanagari for Hindi. Digits are always 0–9, and amounts use the Indian format: ₹1,25,000.00.

5. Every List Works The Same Way

List→Tap A Row→View→Edit→Save

At the top of a list: search, filter, and period (today, this month, this year). At the bottom: that list's main button, such as + New Quotation. On a View screen: Share PDF, Edit, Cancel, and Convert To Bill where it applies (quotations, challans, purchase orders).

7. The Phone's Back Button

  1. Always goes back one screen.
  2. If a sheet or the menu is open, it closes that first.
  3. With an unfinished bill it asks: Keep This Bill As A Draft? A bill is never lost silently.
  4. On Home, pressing it twice closes the app ("Press again to close").

8. Your Decisions (15 Nov 2026)

  1. Getting around: the side menu plus the 3 buttons at the bottom of Home, as shown above. No tab bar at the bottom.
  2. "Coming soon" items (GST, Payment Links and so on) show in the menu with a Soon badge, as on the web.
  3. Home has three tabs: Parties, Bills, Items. Approved.

7.4d: New Sale (New)

The whole bill screen: customer, items, scan, discount and charges, GST, referral commission, import from quotation, batch, last prices, payment received, save and share.

Things To Try (7.4d)

  1. From Home, New Bill → choose a customer → in Add Items tap LED Bulb 3 times → change the quantity and rate.
  2. Tap Save straight away: Discount And Charges opens first (as on the web, it must be checked once).
  3. In the filled-in bill, choose LED batch B-2405 (expired) and save.
  4. In Referral choose Raju Electrician: 5% commission goes into the rate and is not printed.
  5. Payment Received: split between Cash and UPI; paying more clears old dues.
  6. Choose Cash Sale: no customer needed, the full amount counts as received.

What We Need From You (7.4d)

Make a few bills and tell us what to change. After your approval, the next step is 7.4e: quotations, challans, payments, credit notes and commission.

7.4c: Items And Stock (Approved)

The item list, item page, adjusting stock, new item, camera scan, Price Manager (with PIN), bulk active/inactive, brands, godowns and the rate list.

Things To Try (7.4c)

  1. In the list, tap the Low Stock or Out Of Stock box; tap Modular Switch 16A to open its 3 brands.
  2. LED Bulb: in Adjust, reduce 10, watch Stock After, then save.
  3. Price Manager: Price Setup → any 4 digits → tick items → increase 5% → preview → save.
  4. New Item: choose Variant; turn on Pack And Loose; scan the code with the camera button.
  5. Godowns: try to send 200 from Main Shop to Chowk Store Room (there isn't that much).

7.4b: Parties (Approved)

The party list, party page, statement, reminder, and adding or editing a party. Everything the web app has, laid out for the phone.

Things To Try (7.4b)

  1. On Home, tap the You'll Get card: only the parties you get money from.
  2. In the list, try the Customers / Suppliers tabs, the balance filter and search. At the bottom: Show Inactive.
  3. Open Mehta Electricals: the Overdue, Unpaid and Paid filters; Reminder; change the period in Statement.
  4. New Party: tap Save with no name; then pick a contact with 📒; open the three sections below.
  5. Try to delete Mehta: it has bills, so you get Make Inactive. Raju Electrician has no bills and can be deleted.
  6. Import Contacts at the bottom of the list: several contacts become parties at once.
