2. Rules

  1. One meaning per colour: blue = start something, green = finish / money in, red = delete / money out, orange = credit, slate = draft / cancelled.
  2. Every amount also has an arrow or a word: ↓ to get, ↑ to give.
  3. Every button is at least 44 px tall, easy for a finger.
  4. Names (party, supplier, item) always stay on one line; what does not fit ends with "…". Amounts are never cut; they get a little smaller when space is short.
  5. Dates are always written in full: September 25, 2026.
  6. Every word of a button, field, tab, badge or heading starts with a capital letter: Purchase Bill, Take Payment. Full sentences stay normal.
  7. Delete is always light red, never solid red, and always asks first.
  8. Portrait only. Light look only.

3. Lettering

Plus Jakarta Sans for English (the same as the computer app), Noto Sans Devanagari for Hindi. Digits are always 0–9, and amounts use the Indian format: ₹1,25,000.00.

5. Every List Works The Same Way

List→Tap A Row→View→Edit→Save

At the top of a list: search, filter, and period (today, this month, this year). At the bottom: that list's main button, such as + New Quotation. On a View screen: Share PDF, Edit, Cancel, and Convert To Bill where it applies (quotations, challans, purchase orders).

7. The Phone's Back Button

  1. Always goes back one screen.
  2. If a sheet or the menu is open, it closes that first.
  3. With an unfinished bill it asks: Keep This Bill As A Draft? A bill is never lost silently.
  4. On Home, pressing it twice closes the app ("Press again to close").
