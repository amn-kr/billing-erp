BBilling ERP

Privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

This page is general information about how Billing ERP handles your information, written in plain words. It is not legal advice.

In short: Billing ERP keeps your shop’s books for you. We use your information only to run the app for you. We don’t sell it, we don’t show ads, and the app has no tracking tools.

Who we are

Billing ERP is an online app for billing, stock and accounts, run by Aman Kumar in India. For anything about your information, write to amanxbkj@gmail.com.

What we store

  • Your account: your name, email address and password. The password is kept only in scrambled form (hashed with argon2), never as the password itself.
  • Your shop and its books: business details (name, address, phone, GSTIN) and everything you and your staff enter: parties, items, prices, stock, quotations, invoices, purchases, payments, expenses, cash and bank entries.
  • Activity records: who created, changed or cancelled what, and when, with the internet (IP) address it came from; and the devices you are signed in on (browser name and IP address), so you stay signed in.
  • On your device: a sign-in cookie, and a few display choices (such as colours) kept in your browser.

How we use it

Only to run the app for you: to show, print and share your books, to let your staff work in the same books, to make backups and to keep accounts secure. We don’t sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the services below that run the app for us.

Services that run the app for us

  • Render (web hosting, Singapore) runs the app.
  • Neon (database on Amazon Web Services, Singapore) stores your books.
  • GitHub keeps our nightly, encrypted copy of the whole database for 30 days, for emergencies.
  • Google Fonts supplies the app’s letters; Google sees your internet address when they load.
  • Google Drive, only if you choose it for backups (see below).
  • WhatsApp, only when you press a WhatsApp button: the message opens in WhatsApp on your own device.

Backups to Google Drive

  • Only if the shop owner connects Google Drive in Settings › General › Backup & restore.
  • We ask Google for one permission only: files this app creates (Google calls it “drive.file”). The app cannot see, open or change any other file in your Google Drive.
  • The app makes a folder called “Billing ERP Backup – <your shop’s name>” and saves your backups in it. The files are locked (encrypted) so only this app can open them. They stay in your Drive and belong to you.
  • We read your Google account’s email address only to show which account is connected.
  • Google gives the app a sign-in token so it can save backups without asking you every time. We keep it encrypted (AES-256) on our server. It is never shown on screen, written to logs, or shown to staff.
  • You can disconnect at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (remove “Billing ERP”). Your backup files stay in your Drive.
  • Billing ERP’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google Drive only to save and read your backups.

How we keep it safe

Every connection is encrypted (HTTPS). Each shop’s books are kept apart by the database itself, so one shop can never see another shop’s books. Passwords are hashed, and backups and Google sign-in tokens are encrypted.

How long we keep it

As long as your account is open. Invoices and other documents are never deleted from the books, only cancelled, because accounting records have to be kept. Our nightly copies of the database are kept for 30 days. To close your account and have your information deleted, write to the contact email above; we reply within 30 days.

Your choices

You can see and download all your books at any time (Settings › General › Backup & restore › Download backup), correct your details in Settings, and ask us for a copy of your information or for it to be deleted.

Children

The app is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes. For important changes we also tell shop owners in the app.
